Dice notation

How to read and write rolls like tabletop RPGs use

Format

Enter how many dice, the letter d, then how many sides each die has. You can add a flat bonus or penalty with + or - at the end.

  • 3d6 — roll three six-sided dice and sum them.
  • 1d10+2 — one ten-sided die, then add 2.
  • 2d38-1 — two thirty-eight-sided dice, then subtract 1.

Results

The app shows each individual die face, the sum of those dice, any modifier, and the final total. Spaces in your input are ignored.

Limits

To keep rolls fast in the browser, there is a cap on dice count and sides per request. Very large rolls may be rejected with a short error message.
